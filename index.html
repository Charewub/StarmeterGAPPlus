The Training Management Center (TMC) is here to ensure you have a seamless experience managing your Individual Development Plan (IDP) and 2026 performance goals. We encourage you to reach out to our team if you encounter any of the following situations:

Technical Assistance & Access
Contact us if you are unable to log into the StarMeter platform, experience system timeouts or errors while saving your goals, or if your dashboard is not loading correctly.

Performance & Goal Setting Inquiries
Reach out if you need clarification on how to navigate the system to input your 2026 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs), have questions regarding the IDP submission and approval workflow, or need guidance on finding specific learning courses to attach to your development plan.

Data & Profile Corrections
Let us know if your user profile contains incorrect information, such as an outdated job title, incorrect department, or if your assigned reporting manager is incorrect.

How to Reach Us
For any of the scenarios above, please email the Training Management Center (TMC) at taa_learning@airasia.com.
